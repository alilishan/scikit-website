# Frequently Asked Questions

## Websites

**How much does a website cost in Australia?**
Our websites start from $3,500 + GST for a small business site. Sites with bookings or integrations start from $7,500, and online stores from $12,000. Every site includes SEO setup, mobile optimisation, secure hosting setup and backups.

**How long does a website take to build?**
A Starter site is usually live 3 weeks after we receive your content. Larger sites and online stores take 4–8 weeks.

**Do I need to write the content?**
No. Send us the key facts about your business and we'll write and polish the copy.

**Can I update the website myself?**
Yes. You can edit text, photos and blog posts without a developer.

**Will I own my website?**
Yes. The domain, hosting account, code and content are all in your name.

## SEO

**What is SEO and why does my business need it?**
SEO (search engine optimisation) is how you get your website to appear when people search for what you do, on Google and, increasingly, in AI tools like ChatGPT. It decides whether customers find you or a competitor.

**How much does SEO cost?**
Monthly plans start from $790 + GST. A one-off local SEO setup starts from $1,500 + GST.

**How long until I see results?**
Usually 3–6 months for meaningful movement. Google Business Profile and technical fixes can show results within weeks.

**Do you guarantee first-page Google rankings?**
No. No honest agency can guarantee rankings, because Google weighs hundreds of factors. We follow every proven best practice and report your rankings and enquiries every month.

**What is AI search (GEO)?**
More people now ask ChatGPT, Google AI or Perplexity for recommendations instead of scrolling search results. GEO (generative engine optimisation) makes sure those tools can find, understand and recommend your business.
