## FAQs

**What happens to unused hours?**
They roll over for one month, then expire.

**What if I need more work than my plan covers?**
Extra work is billed at our standard rate of $150/hour + GST, or quoted as a fixed-price project if it's larger.

**Can I leave?**
Yes, with 30 days' notice. Everything is already in your name, and we'll help you hand over.
