# One team for software and security

## Who we are

We are a two-person development house in Melbourne. One of us builds software. The other makes sure it runs securely.

Between us, that's 40+ years across software development, cloud infrastructure and cyber security, on large systems for large organisations. Scikit is where we do the same work for small businesses: done properly, priced sensibly, and with no juniors or account managers in between.

## The founders

### Ali Lishan — Co-founder, Software & Product

Ali has 20+ years in software development, specialising in web and mobile applications. As a Chief Software Architect, Ali leads engineering teams from first idea through to launch and beyond — designing the architecture, building the product and mentoring the developers behind it.

Ali's toolkit spans React, Node.js, TypeScript, Python, Java and .NET; microservices and event-driven systems; AWS, Azure and Google Cloud; and DevSecOps practices that keep releases fast and safe. Just as importantly, Ali knows how to turn a messy business process into software people actually enjoy using.

- MSc Information Technology Management — Staffordshire University
- BSc (Hons) Software Engineering — Nottingham Trent University
- 20+ years across web, mobile, cloud-native and AI-integrated applications

**At Scikit, Ali leads:** websites, custom software, mobile apps and AI integrations.

### Hassan Sheikh — Co-founder, Cloud & Security

Hassan has 20 years in infrastructure, cloud and security, and is based in Melbourne. Hassan's career has run from supporting small and medium businesses in regional Queensland, through a national law firm and a mid-market managed service provider, to senior roles at Metro Trains Melbourne and Amazon Web Services — where Hassan worked as a Solutions Architect leading cloud migrations and designing secure, cost-effective architectures.

Today Hassan is a Cloud Solutions Architect in Victoria's electricity distribution sector, designing secure AWS environments, data platforms and generative AI solutions with strict security and compliance requirements.

- AWS Certified Solutions Architect
- AWS Certified Security – Specialty
- VMware Certified Professional (DCV)
- Cisco CCNA
- Microsoft MCITP and MCSE
- Bachelor of Computer Science — La Trobe University

**At Scikit, Hassan leads:** cloud, Microsoft 365, IT, cyber security and infrastructure.
