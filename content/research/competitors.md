# Competitor research: Australian firms similar to Scikit

*Researched September 2026. Descriptions come from each company's own website or directory listings and haven't been independently checked. Visit the sites before relying on any detail.*

## The short version

- **The market splits into two camps, and Scikit sits between them.** Dev studios build websites and software but don't manage IT or security. Managed IT providers (MSPs) handle Microsoft 365, security and support but don't build software. Very few small firms do both at a senior level. That's the gap to own.
- **The closest match on pitch is Blue Frog Software** (a small studio where you deal directly with the people building). **The closest on IT and security is Cyberkite** (Melbourne, businesses with 10 staff or fewer).
- **Pricing check:** the $3.5k starter website and project ranges line up with the market. The **$99/mo Essentials care plan is expensive next to budget studios** (VisualWeb's plans start at $32.95/mo). Either justify it clearly or add a cheaper hosting-only tier.
- **Name watch:** *Cyberkite* is a Melbourne competitor, so it's good you didn't use "Scikite". *scikit-learn* dominates searches for "scikit". See the SEO/GEO strategy for how to handle it.

## Where Scikit can stand out

1. **Software and security under one roof.** Nobody in section 1 leads with AWS security credentials. Nobody in section 4 builds software. Make this the headline everywhere.
2. **Founder-led and senior.** Blue Frog and Dcode also use this. We back it up with named employers (AWS, Metro Trains, Powercor).
3. **Published prices.** Only Melbit and VisualWeb do this among the firms reviewed. It's a real trust signal, so keep it.
4. **AI with guardrails.** AI agencies sell on speed. We can sell on *safe* AI (privacy, guardrails, Australian hosting), which suits clinics, legal and accounting firms.
5. **Micro-business focus (1–50 staff).** AWD starts at 20 staff and the big app agencies price out micro-businesses. Cyberkite is the only direct overlap here.

## Suggested next steps

- Look through Blue Frog's, Cyberkite's and Melbit's sites in detail. Note their offers, guarantees and how they prove they're credible.
- Reconsider the $99 Essentials tier. Options: a $39–49/mo "Hosting & backups" tier, or keep $99 and state what's included that budget plans don't.
- Get listed on the same directories these firms rank through: Clutch, GoodFirms, DesignRush, TechBehemoths. AI search tools cite these heavily (see the SEO/GEO strategy).
