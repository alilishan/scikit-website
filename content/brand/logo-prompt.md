# Logo prompt for Claude Design (v2: web design & SEO positioning)

Paste the block below into Claude Design.

---

Design a logo for **Scikit** (scikit.com.au), a web design and SEO agency in Melbourne, Australia. We build fast, secure websites and run SEO campaigns that get small businesses found on Google and recommended by AI search. What makes us different: the websites are built by senior engineers (a software architect and an AWS security architect), not assembled from templates. Clients include tradies, clinics, cafés, accountants and local shops.

**The idea to express:** *getting found, and rising.* Visibility, climbing the rankings, and a solid, well-engineered foundation underneath.

**Personality:** modern, confident, friendly and precise. Approachable enough for a local café owner, sharp enough to signal real engineering skill. Clean and minimal, with plenty of whitespace, similar in spirit to a modern boutique digital agency. Not corporate, not playful-cartoonish, not "hacker".

**Deliverables:**
1. Primary horizontal lockup: symbol + "Scikit" wordmark
2. Symbol alone (must work at 16×16 px as a favicon and as a social avatar)
3. Wordmark alone
4. Versions for light backgrounds, dark backgrounds and single colour
5. Mock-ups: website header, browser-tab favicon, Google Business Profile avatar (circle crop), business card, Instagram/LinkedIn profile

**Symbol concepts (show 4 distinct directions):**
- **Step up:** an abstract "S" drawn as a single continuous line or path that steps upward from bottom-left to top-right, like a ranking climbing or a route to the top.
- **Found:** a minimal mark combining a location pin, or a search/magnifier idea, with an upward step. Avoid a literal magnifying glass.
- **Stacked blocks / "kit":** three or four clean blocks stepping upward like a bar chart or staircase, suggesting building a site piece by piece and rising.
- **Monogram "S":** a bold, geometric S built from two interlocking shapes (design + engineering), with a subtle upward direction.

**Wordmark:** "Scikit" in sentence case, in a modern geometric or grotesque sans-serif (the feel of Inter, Manrope, General Sans or Satoshi, but with custom touches). One small detail could echo the symbol, such as the dot on the "i" becoming a small square, a block or an upward arrow. It must read instantly as one word, "Scikit", and never as "Sci kit".

**Colour:** propose 2 palettes.
- A: deep ink navy + a bright energetic accent (electric blue, lime or signal green, suggesting "go" and growth)
- B: near-black + a warm Australian accent (wattle gold or sunset coral)
Accents must pass WCAG AA contrast on white where used for text or buttons. The logo must also work in one flat colour.

**Avoid:** Google's colours or anything that looks like a Google product; magnifying-glass and pin clichés used literally; globes, swooshes, lightbulbs, gears, rockets; gradients that fail in single colour; hairline strokes that vanish at small sizes; anything resembling the scikit-learn logo (orange/blue circles); kangaroos, maps or other obvious Australiana; **no kites or kite shapes of any kind** (including diamond shapes on a string).

**Output:** vector-style artwork on a clean presentation board, with palette hex codes, the suggested typeface, and a one-line rationale for each concept. Recommend the strongest concept and explain why.
