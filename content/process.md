# Our Process

Four steps from first chat to a website that brings in work. Fixed prices, weekly updates, no surprises.

---

## 01. Audit

We review your current website, your competitors and what your customers search for on Google. You get a free audit with the three things we'd fix first.

*You get:* a video walkthrough and a one-page report.

## 02. Plan

We send a fixed-price proposal in plain English: a sitemap, the keywords we'll target, the timeline and the cost.

*You get:* a clear scope and price before any work starts.

## 03. Build

We design and develop your site with speed, SEO and security built in. You get a preview link and a weekly update, so you can see progress and give feedback early.

*You get:* a site you've seen and approved at every step.

## 04. Launch & Grow

We go live, submit your site to Google, set up analytics and hand over every login in your name. With an SEO or care plan, we keep improving your rankings and send a monthly report.

*You get:* a live site, full ownership, and results you can track.
